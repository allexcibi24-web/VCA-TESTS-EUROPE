# Original awareness courses – references

The course lessons and questions are original summaries and scenarios, not copies of third-party training manuals.

- UK HSE: https://www.hse.gov.uk/msd/manual-handling/
- UK HSE INDG143: https://www.hse.gov.uk/PUBNS/indg143.htm
- UK HSE HSG17: https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/hsg17.htm
- Ireland HSA: https://www.hsa.ie/workplace_health/manual_handling_display_screen_equipment/faqs/manual_handling_risk_management_faqs/manual_handling/
- UK HSE training competence: https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/training-competence.htm

## Important limitations
- Lessons and exam questions are currently authored in **English**. The new course navigation is translated in RO, EN, FR, DE, NL. Specialist-reviewed full translations are not yet complete.
- Both courses are awareness-only and do not certify practical competence or accreditation.
- 7 lessons, 24-item pool, 20 randomized questions, 80% pass per course.
- Certificates are printable locally; references are not independently verifiable. Do not represent them as accredited.
- Current HTML saves progress only in browser localStorage; no server or identity verification.
